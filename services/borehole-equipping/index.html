A Drilled Borehole Only Becomes a Water Supply Once It's Equipped

Drilling a borehole and reaching water is a major milestone. But until your borehole is properly equipped, that water can't be delivered reliably where you need it.

Turning a drilled borehole into a reliable water supply depends on designing an equipping solution that's matched to both your borehole and your water requirements. This includes selecting and installing the appropriate pump, pipework, and control system for your specific project. Get this stage wrong, and even a borehole with an excellent yield can deliver water unreliably or fail to deliver it altogether.

At Masterpiece Hydro-Solutions, we design and install borehole equipping solutions tailored to your borehole and water requirements, turning a completed borehole into a dependable water supply for everyday use.

Why Proper Borehole Equipping Matters

Once your borehole has been drilled and tested, the next step is equipping it to deliver water where you need it.

The way your borehole is equipped affects how reliably it supplies water, how efficiently the system operates, and how well it performs over time.

Proper borehole equipping involves more than installing a pump. The pump, pipework, control system, and power supply all need to work together as one system that's designed for your borehole and the way you'll use the water.

That's why every equipping decision is based on your borehole's characteristics and your water requirements. A properly designed system protects the investment you've already made in drilling and gives you a dependable water supply for your home, farm, business, or institution.

What Happens During Borehole Equipping?

Before we select or install any equipment, we first make sure we understand your borehole and what you need the water system to do.

  1. 1

    We Review Your Borehole

    We review your drilling completion report and test pumping results to understand your borehole's depth, water level, and how much water it can reliably provide.

  2. 2

    We Understand Your Water Needs

    We discuss how you plan to use the water, how much you need, where it needs to go, and the most suitable way to power your system.

  3. 3

    We Select & Install the Equipment

    Using this information, we select equipment suited to your borehole and water needs. This typically includes the pump, rising mains, cables, control system, and other components needed to deliver water safely and reliably.

  4. 4

    We Test the System

    Once installation is complete, we test and commission the system to make sure everything is working correctly before we hand it over to you.

After Installation, You'll Receive

  • A summary of the equipment installed
  • Guidance on operating your system
  • Guidance on maintaining your system, so you know what to expect from your borehole going forward

What Affects the Cost of Borehole Equipping?

The cost of borehole equipping depends on both your borehole's characteristics and the type of water supply system you need. Rather than offer a single figure that may not reflect your project, we explain the main factors that influence cost so you know what to expect before requesting a quotation.

Borehole Depth and Pumping Depth

The depth of your borehole, together with the depth at which the pump is installed, influences the type of pump required and the amount of rising main, cable, and other materials needed.

Water Requirements

The amount of water you need, how often you'll use it, and what it will be used for all influence the size and capacity of the system required.

Pump Type and Capacity

Different pump types and capacities are suited to different boreholes and water requirements, and this affects both system performance and overall cost.

Power Source

Whether your system is powered by grid electricity, solar, or another suitable power source influences the equipment required and the overall installation cost.

Pipework and Fittings

The distance between the borehole and where the water is needed affects the amount of pipework, cabling, and associated fittings required.

Storage and Distribution

Projects that include water storage tanks or distribution to multiple buildings or outlets require additional equipment and installation work.

Because every project is different, we prepare quotations based on your specific borehole and water requirements rather than standard package pricing.

How Is the Right Pump Selected?

It's natural to assume that a more powerful pump will deliver better performance. In practice, the right pump is not the biggest or most powerful one available. It's the one that's matched to your borehole and your water requirements.

Choosing the right pump involves more than selecting a model from a catalogue. Before recommending a pump, we assess your borehole's characteristics and your water requirements, so the system is designed to deliver reliable performance over the long term.

Our recommendation takes into account factors such as:

  • Your borehole's depth and water level.
  • How much water your borehole can reliably provide.
  • How much water you need each day.
  • How you'll use the water, whether for a home, farm, business, or institution.
  • How far the water needs to be pumped.
  • Whether the water is salty or fresh.

By assessing these factors together, we recommend a pump that's suited to both your borehole and your water requirements. This means your system delivers the water you need while operating reliably and efficiently for years to come.
